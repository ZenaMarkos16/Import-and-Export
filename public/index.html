Inquiry privacy notice

When you submit an inquiry, this website records your name, email, company (if supplied), inquiry type, message, consent, and submission time to handle your request.

Inquiries are stored on the website server and are not publicly accessible. No marketing subscription is created and no automatic email is sent. Avoid including sensitive personal or financial information.

For a request about your submission, use the inquiry form and include your reference number. Production retention and contact policies must be confirmed by Zena before public launch.
